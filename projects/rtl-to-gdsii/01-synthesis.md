# Synthesis

## Tool

Cadence Genus

## Objective

Convert the RTL design into a gate-level netlist while applying the required timing constraints and analyzing area, timing, and power.

## Technology

* 28nm
* Standard-cell types: HVT, SVT, LVT

## Work Performed

* RTL loading and elaboration
* SDC constraint application
* Gate-level synthesis
* Timing analysis
* Area analysis
* Power analysis
* VT-cell analysis
* Timing optimization
* Buffer insertion and gate sizing

## Outcome

A gate-level netlist and synthesis reports were generated successfully for the design.
