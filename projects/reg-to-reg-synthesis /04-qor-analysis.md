# QoR Analysis

## Tool

Synopsys Design Compiler

## Objective

Analyze the overall quality of the synthesized design using the available Quality of Results (QoR) information.

## QoR Analysis

The `report_qor` command was used to generate the synthesis QoR report.

The QoR report provides information related to:

* Area
* Utilization
* Power consumption
* Timing constraints
* Critical paths
* Optimization results

## Key Command

```tcl
report_qor
```

## Analysis

The QoR report was used together with timing reports to evaluate the quality of the synthesized design and identify areas requiring further optimization.

## Outcome

QoR information was generated after synthesis to evaluate the design across area, timing, power, and optimization-related metrics.
